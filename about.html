WHY CASHGLADE EXISTS

A clearer way to plan student life.

Cashglade is a budgeting and spending-planning app for students. It brings available cash, upcoming bills, expected income, savings plans and recorded purchases into one manually entered plan.

Its purpose is practical: help you understand what is left after the essentials, check a purchase before recording it, and keep your plan in step with your actual balance. There’s also an everyday-life mode for planning around paydays and household costs.

Cashglade is being prepared for its first iPhone release. A November 1, 2026 public launch is a target, subject to testing and App Store approval.
