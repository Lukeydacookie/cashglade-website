Guidance reflects the reviewed 0.4.2 source. Some device behavior remains subject to release testing.

Does Cashglade connect to my bank?

No. The reviewed version uses manual entries and has no bank connection or Cashglade cloud account. Use your actual spendable cash, not a credit limit.

How do I keep a backup?

Open My plan and choose Share backup JSON. Save the readable JSON somewhere private that you can access later. Up to five previous saves may be kept locally, but size limits or failed writes can leave fewer. Export before deleting the app or changing phones.

How do I restore a budget?

In My plan, use Restore previous save for available on-device recovery copies, or Paste a backup for Cashglade JSON. Review the replacement confirmation carefully. A restore replaces the current plan. Keep a separate export first when possible.

What if Cashglade says a change was not saved?

Do not assume it saved. Keep your current data and any exported backup. Follow the error message; if it says the saved data changed, close and reopen before editing again. For a very large budget, export a copy before reducing entries. Avoid uninstalling or clearing the app to troubleshoot a saving problem.

What if my budget does not reopen?

Cashglade may recover an available previous save and display a notice. Review the figures before continuing. If it cannot reopen storage, restart the app as instructed before entering a new budget. Do not uninstall or delete data while trying to preserve it. Contact support with the error wording and sample-only details.

What does “Check my actual balance” do?

Use it to reconcile your plan with your actual balance. Select schedules already included in that balance to avoid counting them twice. Mark paid or received when the transaction has not already been counted in the current Cashglade balance.

Can I use it offline or on another phone?

Core budgeting works offline. There is no automatic cloud budget sync. To move a plan, first save a private JSON export and restore that backup on the other device when Cashglade is available there.

How do I delete my financial information?

Open My plan → Delete all data and start again, then confirm. Successful deletion removes the current budget, recovery copies and unfinished setup. The mode preference may remain. Exports, emails, other recipients’ copies and operating-system backups need to be deleted separately.

Can I try it without entering private figures?

The app includes Explore a sample budget. Sample exploration does not overwrite your saved real budget. The website has no budget-entry form or online budget storage.

What should I include in a support email?

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Cashglade version/build if known, the exact error text, what you expected and the steps that led to it. Use sample amounts and redact screenshots. Never send bank passwords, card numbers or a complete financial backup. No response-time guarantee is offered.

Keep a copy before you troubleshoot.

Recovery saves live on the same device as your budget. Do not delete the app, clear its data, or restore another plan while trying to preserve a budget without first keeping an export where possible.
