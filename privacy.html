01

Operator and contact

Cashglade is operated by Lukasz M Stanczyk, who handles customer support and privacy requests. Contact cashglade.support@gmail.com for assistance or privacy inquiries.

02

What the app stores

You manually enter available cash, a cushion to keep untouched, planning dates, named bills and expected income with amounts and schedules, purchases, savings-transfer plans and balance-reconciliation information. Cashglade also stores unfinished setup and your student or everyday-life mode preference.

The reviewed version stores this information in ordinary app storage on your iPhone using AsyncStorage. Core budgeting works offline. The app does not connect to bank accounts or require a Cashglade cloud account. Do not enter bank passwords, account numbers or card details.

03

Collection and sharing

The reviewed app code does not upload your budget to a Cashglade server and includes no advertising, analytics or user-tracking integration. It uses your entries on the device to calculate and display your plan. Local budget entries are not sold or used for advertising.

Sharing a backup, contacting support, visiting this website and using Apple’s distribution/device services involve separate data flows described below. The final signed iPhone build and third-party SDK behavior have not yet been verified; this policy does not claim that verification is complete.

04

Backups, retention and deletion

Recovery storage keeps up to five previous saves, with fewer possible because of budget size or storage errors. These local copies rotate as you save and are not a separate backup. Current plans remain until replaced or deleted; setup remains until successfully cleared. Operating-system backups may retain app data according to your settings.

Share backup JSON sends a readable, unencrypted copy through the iOS share sheet to the app or recipient you choose. That recipient controls its copy. Paste a backup validates Cashglade JSON and asks you to confirm replacement of the current plan; Restore previous save uses available local recovery copies. There is no automatic cloud budget sync.

To remove financial information, open My plan → Delete all data and start again and confirm. Successful deletion removes the current budget, recovery copies and unfinished setup; the nonfinancial mode preference may remain. A deletion error means you should not assume it completed. Delete exported files, recipients’ copies, emails and device backups separately. Export privately before uninstalling or changing phones.

05

Support correspondence

If you email cashglade.support@gmail.com, we receive your address, message and any attachments you choose to send. Gmail processes the correspondence under Google’s privacy policy. We use support information to answer your request and address the issue. Use sample amounts and redact screenshots; do not send financial backups or credentials.

We retain support correspondence for up to 90 days after the issue is resolved, unless a longer period is legally required. You can request earlier deletion through the support email, subject to applicable retention requirements. Google may retain service backups under its own policies. Deleting a local budget does not delete earlier emails.

06

Website, libraries and other services

This website has no own analytics, advertising, contact form, embedded social feeds, external font service, budget storage or cookies set by its code. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives ordinary requests and logs visitor IP addresses for security under its Pages documentation and privacy statement. The website does not receive your budget.

The app uses React Native, Expo, AsyncStorage and associated native libraries. Required-reason privacy declarations cover file timestamps, user defaults, boot time and disk space; they are not tracking consent. No camera, microphone, location, contacts or photo-library usage descriptions were present in the reviewed unsigned simulator build. Final signed-build SDK/permission behavior remains unverified.

Apple may process distribution, TestFlight, backup or diagnostic information under its policies and your settings. Other apps or providers you choose for email, links or exports handle that interaction under their own policies.

07

Security and children’s privacy

App storage is not an encrypted vault and exported backups are readable. Protect your device with a passcode, keep software current and share backups carefully. No storage or communication method is guaranteed secure.

Cashglade is intended primarily for high school students, college students and adults. It is not specifically directed to children under 13. The app does not ask for a date of birth or verify age. If you believe a child sent personal information to support, contact cashglade.support@gmail.com without sending additional sensitive details.

08

Changes and privacy requests

We will update this policy and its effective date when practices change, and explain material changes where required. Contact cashglade.support@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests concerning information held through support. Your on-device budget is unavailable to support unless you choose to send it. Depending on your location, you may have additional privacy rights. Contact us to exercise applicable rights or raise a concern.
