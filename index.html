A PLAN THAT STARTS WITH YOUR LIFE

From “can I afford it?”
to a clearer picture.

01 / START WITH TODAY

Know your starting point.

Add the cash you can actually spend, a cushion to keep untouched, and the date you’re planning through.

02 / MAKE SPACE FOR ESSENTIALS

Give the important things a place.

Plan bills and expected income by date. Reserve savings transfers and record purchases as life happens.

03 / LOOK AHEAD

Check before you commit.

See a spending allowance and forecast. Preview a purchase before recording it, and reconcile your plan with your actual balance.

Cashglade’s estimates depend on your entries and expected income. They are planning tools, not guarantees or financial advice.
